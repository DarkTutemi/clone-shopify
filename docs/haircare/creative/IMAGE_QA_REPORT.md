## 1. Compliance Checklist (Section 6 Criteria)
Each candidate was rigorously checked against the following failure criteria:
1. **Packaging Accuracy:** Packshot labels are NOT faked or hallucinated by AI; original authenticated packshots are preserved for buy boxes, while AI photography provides editorial lifestyles, ingredient macros, and sensorial usage.
2. **Anatomical Correctness:** Hands, fingers, and facial proportions inspected. Zero extra digits, distorted joints, or warped fingernails.
3. **Texture Realism:** Hair strands display authentic curl clumps, cuticle sheen, natural frizz-resistance, and realistic density without plastic/greasy sheen or CGI blur.
4. **No Misleading Claims:** No fake clinical regrowth, no deceptive split-screen before-and-after transformations, no invented scientific patents.
5. **Baked-in Text Check:** Zero garbled text, false brand watermarks, or pseudo-labels baked into image pixels.
6. **Negative Space & Layout Fit:** All banners offer adequate negative space matching the ShopSideK T01 typography grid.
