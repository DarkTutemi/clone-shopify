### Mandatory Testing & Regulatory Requirements:
1. **Preservative Efficacy Testing (PET / USP <51>):** Passing criteria required for aqueous formulations (P06, P08, P03).
2. **Microbiological Examination (USP <61> & <62>):** Absence of S. aureus, P. aeruginosa, C. albicans, E. coli. Total aerobic microbial count < 100 CFU/g.
3. **Accelerated Stability (3 Months at 40°C / 75% RH & Freeze-Thaw 3 Cycles):** Zero emulsion separation, precipitation, viscosity collapse, or fragrance rancidity.
4. **Container Compatibility:** Zero leaching, stress cracking, or discoloration of amber glass, aluminum, or HDPE containers.
5. **Heavy Metal Testing:** Pb < 10 ppm, As < 3 ppm, Cd < 3 ppm, Hg < 1 ppm.
