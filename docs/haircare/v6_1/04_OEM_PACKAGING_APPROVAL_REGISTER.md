# ZAVELYN HAIR CARE V6.1 — OEM PACKAGING APPROVAL REGISTER

## Policy & Production Governance
To prevent the recurrence of inventory and spec contradictions (such as the 100g balm vs 20g physical stock, or squeeze tube vs pump bottle), this register establishes the formal boundary between **Current Physical Inventory** and **Future OEM Concept R&D**.

No speculative OEM specifications may be deployed customer-facing on Shopify live or draft themes without explicit, signed sign-off from the store owner.
