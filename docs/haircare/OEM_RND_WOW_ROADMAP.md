## 1. PURPOSE & REGULATORY SEPARATION PRINCIPLE

Under FTC (16 CFR Part 255) and FDA cosmetic regulations (FD&C Act Chapter VI), cosmetic storefronts are strictly prohibited from marketing unverified medical, drug, or clinical performance claims as present facts without prior competent and reliable scientific evidence (CARSE).

This document serves as the **strict firewall** between:
1. **VERIFIED NOW (Storefront-Permitted Claims):** Immediate cosmetic benefits observable from physical properties (lubrication, moisture retention, surface shine, cosmetic coverage, detangling ease, comb-force reduction).
2. **PROPOSED OEM (Future Laboratory Goals):** R&D efficacy benchmarks requiring standardized clinical or instrumental testing protocols before publication.
3. **UNVERIFIED / BLOCKED (Strictly Prohibited):** Anecdotal folk-lore claims, hair follicle regeneration, curing alopecia, or fabricated percentage improvements.

## 2. TRI-TIER EVIDENCE CLASSIFICATION MATRIX

| Tier | Classification | Definition | Storefront Permitted? | Required Substantiation Dossier |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier A** | `VERIFIED NOW` | Immediate tactile, cosmetic, sensory, and aesthetic benefits based on verified ingredient properties and physical mechanisms. | **YES** | Standard INCI documentation, certificate of analysis (COA), safety data sheet (SDS), physical mechanism logic. |
| **Tier B** | `PROPOSED OEM` | Measurable efficacy improvements targeted for OEM production runs (e.g. 72h frizz control, 85% breakage reduction, scalp sebum balance). | **NO** (Until tested) | Double-blind clinical panel ($n \ge 30$), tress instrumental comb-force testing, certified lab study report. |
| **Tier C** | `UNVERIFIED` | Drug-like claims such as "cures balding", "reverses alopecia in 14 days", "regenerates dormant follicles". | **STRICTLY PROHIBITED** | IND/NDA drug approval, FDA clearance. Never permitted for cosmetic storefronts. |

- **Verified Now (Storefront):**

  - Imparts natural high-gloss shine and enhances softness.
  - Lubricates hair cuticles, reducing mechanical friction during brushing.
  - Scent: Characteristic earthy, smoky, nutty roasted batana aroma.
- **Proposed OEM Research Targets (Lab Testing Needed):**
