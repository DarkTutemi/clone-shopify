Any Questions?
Let us answer them!

Will this help with hyperpigmentation and melasma?

The antioxidants in turmeric help reduce hyperpigmentation and melasma, making skin appear more even-toned over time.

How long before I see results?

Many customers notice a brighter complexion within 7-14 days, with dark spots fading significantly in 30 days. Consistency is key!

How do I use the Turmeric Bar for the best results?

Face: Lather, apply for 60-90 seconds, then rinse. Follow with a natural moisturizer or butter such as shea butter.

Body: Use daily in the shower, focusing on dark spots or uneven areas (e.g., underarms, bikini line, knees).

Can I use this bar every day?

Yes! It’s gentle enough for daily use, but if you have sensitive skin, start with every other day and increase as needed.

Is this safe for sensitive skin?

Yes! Our formula is 100% natural, sulfate-free, and gentle. However, if you have extremely sensitive skin, we recommend a patch test first.

Can I use this on my bikini line or underarms?

Absolutely! Many customers love using the Turmeric Glow Bar to fade dark spot sin the bikini area, underarms, knees, and elbows.

Will the turmeric stain my skin or clothes?

No! Our formula ensures zero staining on your skin. If any residue transfers to fabrics, it washes out easily.

How long does one bar last?

Face use only: 4-6 weeks

Face & body use: 3-4 weeks

(Pro tip: Store in a dry soap dish to extend lifespan!)

What if it doesn't work for me?

We offer a 100% money-back guarantee. If you’re not happy with your results after 60 days, contact us for a full refund.

Will this lighten my natural skin tone?

No, our Turmeric Glow Soap does not lighten your natural skin tone. It works by fading dark spots, evening out discoloration, and restoring your skin’s natural glow without altering your original complexion.

Turmeric is known for its brightening properties, which means it helps reduce hyperpigmentation, sun spots, and blemishes—but it will not bleach or lighten beyond your natural skin color.

Your skin stays beautifully radiant, balanced, and even-toned—just as nature intended. 🌿✨
