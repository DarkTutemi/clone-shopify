GENTLE POWERED FLOW

Unlike harsh squeeze bottles, Navage's generates a smooth, controlled flow with no added pressure during the rinse cycle — making it comfortable enough for daily use, even for first-timers.

HANDHELD DESIGN

A compact handheld design means no leaning over a sink, no managing hoses, no awkward positioning. Stand upright, rinse in seconds, and get on with your day — even while traveling.

PRE-MEASURED SALTPODS
